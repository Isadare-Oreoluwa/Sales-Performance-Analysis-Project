# 📈 Sales Performance Analysis Project

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data across various products, countries, and customer segments. Using a multi-tool approach, I conducted comprehensive analysis in **Microsoft Excel**, **SQL**, and **Power BI** to uncover insights into profitability, cost efficiency, revenue trends, and business performance.

The objective of the project was to demonstrate an end-to-end analytics workflow—from data cleaning and exploration to advanced SQL analysis and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, and pivot tables.
- **SQL** – Advanced analytical queries, aggregations, trend analysis, and business insights.
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and visual storytelling.

---

## ❓ Key Questions Explored

- Which products and countries contribute the most to revenue and profit?
- How does discounting affect sales and profitability?
- Are there trends in cost and revenue per unit over time?
- Which customer segments generate the highest profit margins?
- How do profitability and sales performance vary across products and markets?

---

## 📊 Insights Summary

- Discounts have a strong positive correlation with sales (**0.74**) and a moderate positive correlation with profit (**0.44**).
- **Paseo** generated the highest sales volume but only an average profit margin, indicating potential cost inefficiencies.
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) per unit remained relatively stable over time, while changes in gross profit margin were primarily driven by revenue fluctuations.
- Gross profit margins varied more across customer segments than products, suggesting differences in pricing strategies and cost structures.
- Geographic and segment analysis revealed clear opportunities for improving profitability through targeted pricing and sales strategies.
